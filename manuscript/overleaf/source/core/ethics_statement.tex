\chapter*{Ethics Statement}
This thesis uses previously collected EEG recordings. The NEMAR/OpenNeuro source cohort is openly distributed with dataset attribution; CAUEEG is a controlled-access academic dataset, and its source provider requires an application and applicable ethics approval \cite{caueegofficial}. The separate Isfahan investigation uses de-identified research records. No new recruitment, intervention, patient consent collection or project-specific ethics approval number is claimed by this thesis. We do not publish restricted patient-level CAUEEG raw traces, individual ages, or recording identifiers in the public manuscript.
\par\medskip
EEGraft outputs are research classifications, not medical diagnoses, probabilities of future disease, or treatment decisions. The source models, reported exploratory analyses, synthetic perturbations and electrode occlusion are not validated for clinical decision-making. Any public release of restricted data or artifacts must be checked against the providers' permissions before publication.
