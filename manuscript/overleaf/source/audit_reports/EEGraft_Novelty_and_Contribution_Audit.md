# EEGraft Research Novelty and Contribution Audit

## Verdict

The defensible novelty is the **specific implemented frozen-CBraMod dual-scale residual decoder and a linked correctness/threshold/transfer evaluation on the documented cohorts**. Published components, generic subject-wise validation, threshold selection, calibration, multi-scale convolutions and cross-dataset EEG studies precede EEGraft. A search of closely related literature available through November 2026 did **not** establish universal first-of-its-kind status, so no such claim was added.

## Defensible novelty sentence used in the thesis

> EEGraft implements and evaluates a specific frozen-CBraMod, gated dual-scale residual decoder and uses separate source, external, temporal and negative-control experiments to show how correctness, apparent consistency, threshold rules and cohort effects can disagree.

The thesis does **not** claim to be the first EEG dementia classifier, first multi-scale EEG architecture, first cross-dataset foundation-model experiment, or first probability-calibration study. The available literature scan was targeted to close precedents rather than an exhaustive systematic review of every indexed study through November 2026.

## Value for future research

Future research teams can reuse the evaluation pattern: define participants vs recordings, precommit the primary threshold, retain a locked test, report stable incorrect labels, show exploratory threshold/calibration alternatives separately, and test age/condition confounding. These are evidence-supported methodological practices; they are not promises of patient-level diagnosis, treatment or disease-risk prediction.
