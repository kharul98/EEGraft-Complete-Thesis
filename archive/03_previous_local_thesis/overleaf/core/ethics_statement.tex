\chapter*{Ethics Statement}
The primary EEG experiments use a publicly released, anonymized research dataset described by Miltiadous and colleagues \cite{miltiadous2023dataset,miltiadous2026dataset}. The project analyzes existing recordings and does not report recruitment, clinical intervention, or direct contact with patients. Dataset access and redistribution must continue to follow the dataset's current license and institutional rules. The source publication reports anonymization and participant-related safeguards; this thesis does not claim to have conducted a separate ethics review or obtained new patient consent.\par
\vspace{0.3cm}
\noindent The reported classifications are research labels, not medical diagnoses. Channel-importance plots are model-sensitivity analyses and are not evidence of causal brain biomarkers. Individual predictions should not be used for clinical decisions. No patient names, identifying images, or raw personal health files are included in this Overleaf report package.\par
