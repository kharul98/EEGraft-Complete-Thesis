# 07. Once-only locked holdout

Fixed DSRF and XGBoost models were evaluated once on 13 held-out people (7 AD, 6 CN). DSRF BAcc = 70.24%, XGBoost BAcc = 77.38%. The small exploratory paired interval includes zero. Do not claim DSRF won or clinical diagnostic accuracy.
