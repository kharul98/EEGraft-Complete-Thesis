# 06. Artificial perturbations and model sensitivity

Five seeded noise/channel-removal repeats were performed on the same development subjects. For DSRF, 10% synthetic noise gave mean 71.87% BAcc and 20% gave 70.07%; one-channel zeroing gave 76.78%. Channel-occlusion scores describe model reliance on inputs, not physiological biomarkers.
