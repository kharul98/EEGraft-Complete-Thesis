# 02. Recording-duration and time-anchor analysis

The duration track compared recording segments such as 30, 60, 120, 180 and 300 seconds and sensitivity to segment location. Differences must be tied to their exact analytic cohort, folds and time anchors. Report an observed peak without claiming a universal optimal EEG duration.
