# 05. Development model comparison and ablation

Five subject-disjoint development folds compared eight model/ablation variants. DSRF pooled development balanced accuracy was 77.06%, and XGBoost was 76.61%. DSRF and XGBoost each correctly classified 40 of 52 development subjects. The residual versus gated-blend comparison was exploratory, not conclusive.
