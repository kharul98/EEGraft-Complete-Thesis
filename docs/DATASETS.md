## Additional external datasets

These include datasets used in external feasibility
work and subsequent CAUEEG transfer experiments.

For each dataset, record:

- Official source URL and dataset identifier.
- Access approval and permitted uses.
- Acquisition date and version.
- Raw-file inventory and authorized checksums.
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- Dataset split and participant identity rules.
- Associated scripts, runs, and output locations.

Never substitute recording counts for participant counts.

## Data policy

Raw recordings and participant-identifiable information
are excluded from the public archive by default.

Restricted datasets must be retained in authorized,
access-controlled storage with recovery instructions.
