# EEGraft Research Timeline

## November 6, 2026: Early feasibility

Initial feasibility work considered DARWIN handwriting data.
The project subsequently prioritized EEG-based disease
classification and reliability research.

## November 6-7: EEG research foundation

Work included dataset feasibility, recording-duration
investigations, spectral baselines, subject-level
cross-validation, and robustness-related experiments.

The original research repository records the detailed
progression and executed outputs.

## November 7: Independent MCI analysis

The Isfahan MCI EEG investigation examined classification
performance and prediction stability on an independent
disease task.

The historical negative-control outcomes remain separate
from Alzheimer's disease classification results.

## November 7: Foundation model and decoder development

A pretrained CBraMod EEG model was used for representation
extraction. Custom dual-scale residual fusion decoder
experiments, baseline comparisons, ablations, and
reliability experiments were performed.

The November 7 `PROJECT_HISTORY.md` records historical
results and important methodological boundaries.

## November 7: Locked holdout assessment

The DSRF decoder and XGBoost were compared on the
original locked AD-versus-control holdout.

Historical results must not be altered when later
datasets are incorporated.

## November 8-9: Additional external EEG work

Additional EEG dataset work, including the CAUEEG
transfer investigation, was conducted after the
November 7 GitHub backup.

Imported scripts, model checkpoints, predictions, run
configurations, and output logs are the authoritative
evidence for what was executed.

Do not claim new results or completed independent
validation unless their source files have been checked.

## November 9: Repository consolidation

The earlier repositories and available newer local
working files were organized into one research archive.

This consolidation is an archival operation, not a
new experimental execution.

## Open verification item

A historical duration-audit discrepancy reported
1,380 rows where 690 were expected.

The cause must be reconciled against the source
manifest and counting logic before the duration audit
is described as resolved.
